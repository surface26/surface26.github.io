Why Surface 26

Large-format porcelain is a specialist material.

Most tile and stone shops don't focus exclusively on large format porcelain. We set out to be specialists in this lane. We only do large format porcelain, and we've built our team and process around getting it right.

What you can expect from us

Specialized craftsmanship

Large format porcelain is all we do, and we take that further than most. Our site prep crew only does site prep. Our measure techs only measure. Our install crews only install. Nobody is switching between trades or learning on your project — every person who touches it does that one job, all day, every day. You are the one who benefits from that.

Communication

We tell you what's happening and when, in plain language, without needing to be chased for updates.

Attention to details

We check measurements, seams and layout twice before anything is final, because the small things are what people notice.

Respect for your home

We protect floors and furniture, keep the site clean, and clean up at the end of each day.

Direct answers

If something about your project is unusual or difficult, we'll tell you plainly, not after the fact.

Accountability

We treat every job like our name is on it. It is.
