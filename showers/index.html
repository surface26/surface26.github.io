Large format porcelain showers

A shower should be easy to enjoy and easy to clean. Large format porcelain lets the pattern run across your walls with fewer interruptions and far fewer grout lines to scrub. You get the look you want, with less upkeep.

Custom built to your space

We fabricate porcelain walls, niches, benches and curbs to fit your shower and how you use it. Niches for the bottles you actually use. A bench with room to sit.

See the layout before we cut

We use digital measurements of your shower to plan the cuts, seams and veining. You’ll review and approve the layout before fabrication, so you can see how the pattern comes together across your walls.

Can you use porcelain on the shower floor?

Yes. We’ll help you choose a finish suited to wet areas and plan the floor around your shower’s slope and drain.

How do I clean a porcelain shower?

Warm water, mild soap and a soft cloth are usually all you need. With far fewer grout lines, there’s less scrubbing to keep up with. For specific cleaners and spills, see our Care page.

Do you install shower glass or doors?

We don’t install shower glass or doors, but we have trusted partners we can recommend for your project.
