Large format porcelain fireplaces

A fireplace is already a focal point. Large format porcelain gives it the look of stone, with large panels that let the pattern run from hearth to ceiling. Fewer seams keep the attention on the design.

Cut to fit your fireplace

We measure your wall and cut the porcelain to fit around your fireplace opening. For designs with a hearth or wrapped sides, we can carry the same porcelain across those surfaces for a consistent look.

Can I mount a TV above the fireplace?

Yes, where the fireplace manufacturer’s clearance requirements allow it. We plan and cut the openings for mounting hardware and wiring. Your TV installer handles the mount and TV installation.

Do you do outdoor fireplaces?

Yes. We fabricate and install porcelain surrounds for outdoor fireplaces, with the same options for hearths and wrapped sides.
