Porcelain countertops and vanities

Outdoor kitchens need surfaces that can handle everyday use and the weather. Porcelain is non-porous and UV resistant, making it a great choice for outdoor countertops. It’s easy to clean, never needs sealing and resists fading in the sun.

Carry the look beyond the countertop

Continue the same porcelain up the backsplash or down the sides of an island with waterfall edges. We’ll plan the seams and veining with you, and you’ll approve the layout before anything is cut.

Can my backsplash match my countertop?

Yes. We can use the same porcelain for your countertop and backsplash. Some designs also let us carry the veining from one surface to the next.

What edge options do you offer?

The options depend on the porcelain and thickness you choose. We’ll walk through the details with you and recommend an edge that fits the design.

Do you install sinks or connect plumbing?

We fabricate the sink opening, but we don’t handle plumbing connections. We have trusted partners we can recommend for your project.

Do you work with granite, quartz or quartzite?

Large format porcelain is all we do. For granite, quartz or quartzite, we recommend Surface Providers. They’re a family-owned countertop company in Alpharetta with over 90 years of combined experience. We work out of their building, so if your project includes both stone and porcelain, you’ll have both teams under one roof.
