About Surface 26

Surface 26 is a family business started by two brothers. Our dad built his career in stone surfacing, and we grew up around it — in the shops, around the slabs, on job sites. Surface 26 carries that same craft into large format porcelain, a material most of the industry still treats as an afterthought. We do this work and nothing else.

This trade has a reputation problem, and a lot of it is earned. Dates slip. Calls don't get returned. Homeowners are left guessing what comes next. We think that's a low bar, and clearing it isn't complicated: show up when we say we will, tell you what's happening before you have to ask, and leave behind work we'd put in our own homes. That's the whole idea.

We're from here. We grew up around Atlanta, went to UGA, and came back home to build something. Every job we do carries our last name on it, so we care — a lot. That's not a strategy, it's just how we were raised.

Our mission

We exist to take care of people and best in class porcelain craftsmanship is how we prove it. We do what we say, we care about the details, and we treat every job like our name is on it.

Our fabrication partner

Surface Providers

We work out of Surface Providers' building in Alpharetta, a family-owned countertop company with over 90 years of combined experience across their team. They cut every panel on a robotic waterjet.

They're the best at fabrication. We're the best at consultation and installation. The partnership means you get both, and the project is what benefits.
