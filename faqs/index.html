FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is large format porcelain?

It goes by several names depending on who's talking about it: porcelain slabs, porcelain panels, large format porcelain (LFP), gauged porcelain, thin tile, and sometimes sintered stone. The formal industry term is gauged porcelain tile panels (GPTP).

Where can porcelain be used?

Anywhere you'd use tile or stone: wall cladding, shower and wet-area surrounds, fireplace surrounds, flooring, countertops and vanities, and outdoor kitchens and patios.

How do I prepare for the measure?

The substrate needs to be prepped and ready — walls or cabinets in place, framing finished, plumbing roughed in. We take the digital measure once that prep work is done.

Do you handle plumbing or electrical?

No, we don't do plumbing or electrical ourselves. We have trusted partners we work with and will coordinate them as part of your project.

Do you work with granite, quartz or quartzite?

We don't fabricate stone ourselves. For granite, quartz or quartzite, we'll refer you to Surface Providers, our fabrication partner in Alpharetta.

How is porcelain fabricated?

Panels are cut to your exact layout by Surface Providers, our fabrication partner in Alpharetta, on their robotic waterjet.

Can porcelain be used outdoors?

Yes. It's UV stable, so color doesn't fade in direct sun, and it handles rain and freeze-thaw cycles without issue.

Does porcelain need sealing?

No. Unlike natural stone, it never needs to be sealed.

How is porcelain different from ceramic tile?

It's denser and far larger in format — a single panel can cover a wall or counter that would otherwise take dozens of tiles and grout lines.
