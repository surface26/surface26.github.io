Matching

Pattern matching

How the panels line up decides how the finished wall reads. We plan this before anything is cut, and you approve the layout first.

Book match

Two panels cut in sequence and opened like a book. One is a mirror image of the other, so the veins meet near the seam and form a symmetrical pattern. Best for feature walls, fireplace surrounds and shower back walls.

Vein match

Panels cut side by side from the same slab and set in the same order. The veins carry across the seam close to where they left off, so the wall reads as one continuous surface instead of a mirror image.

Vein flow

The veins on every panel run in the same direction, but they do not line up at the seams. The room has one consistent movement without the cost and slab waste of a matched layout.

Not every color can be book matched or vein matched. Each color above lists what it offers.

Matched seams line up closely, but never perfectly. Porcelain veining is printed and every slab varies slightly, so expect small shifts where panels meet.

Details

Edge finishes

Every outside corner and exposed edge gets finished one of two ways: a mitered return in porcelain, or a metal profile.

Mitered return

Both panels are cut at 45° and joined at the corner, so the porcelain wraps the edge with no metal showing. On certain projects the veins can carry around the corner from one face to the next. The cleanest look.

Schluter profile

A slim metal profile caps the edge where two panels meet. It protects the edge and adds a fine metal line at the corner. Good where the trim matches plumbing fixtures or hardware. There are many colors to choose from, and we will help you pick the right one.

Showers

Niches

Every niche is lined in the same porcelain as the wall, with mitered edges. We have standard sizes, and custom sizes are available.

Single

One recessed niche, lined in the same porcelain as the wall. Sized to hold standard bottles.

Shelf

One tall niche split by a porcelain shelf. Gives multiple levels of storage in a single opening.

Long horizontal

A wide, low niche that runs most of the wall. Reads as a clean line across the shower.

Full length

One recessed horizontal niche the entire length of the wall.

Full length shelf

One open shelf that runs the full length of the wall.

Showers

Benches

Shower benches in the same porcelain as the walls, built with the shower so the waterproofing runs under and behind them.

Floating bench

A thick porcelain seat that cantilevers off the wall with nothing underneath. Light and open, and easy to clean below.

  • Depth Typically 15–18 in
  • Support Hidden steel frame

Full-width bench

A built-in bench that runs wall to wall. The seat and front are wrapped in porcelain with mitered edges.

  • Depth Typically 15–18 in
  • Support Framed and waterproofed with the shower

Corner seat

A triangular seat set into a shower corner. Takes up little floor space and works in smaller showers.

  • Size Typically 16–20 in per side
  • Support Floating or fully supported
Vanities

Integrated sinks

Made from the same porcelain as your countertop, an integrated sink carries the porcelain right into the basin for one cohesive look. Choose from two drain styles.

Linear drain vanity

A single porcelain surface slopes toward a concealed drain at the back of the basin for a clean, uninterrupted look.

Point drain vanity

Four porcelain surfaces slope toward a central drain, with diagonal seams meeting at the drain.

Fireplaces

Electric fireplaces

Electric fireplaces we supply, set into a full porcelain surround. No venting or gas line needed.

Spectrum Slimline

A slender ribbon of flame framed by clean, minimal lines. Detailed driftwood logs and independently controlled flame and ember colors make a refined focal point within a porcelain surround.

  • Lengths 50, 60, 74 and 100 in
  • Height 17 in
  • Depth 4¾ in
  • Heater 5,000 BTU
  • Colors Orange and blue

Landscape Pro

An expansive flame display with hand-painted driftwood logs and customizable lighting. Single-sided, corner and three-sided configurations bring an architectural presence to interior spaces and covered outdoor living areas.

  • Lengths 44, 56, 68, 80, 96 and 120 in
  • Height 25 in
  • Depth 12 in
  • Heater 5,000 and 10,000 BTU
  • Colors Orange, red, pink, purple, green and blue

Redstone

Classic fireplace proportions with a generous, taller flame display. Driftwood logs, crystal embers and adjustable lighting pair naturally with a tailored porcelain surround for an inviting centerpiece with timeless character.

  • Lengths 26, 30, 36, 42 and 54 in
  • Height 18–33 in, depending on length
  • Depth 10–12 in, depending on length
  • Heater 5,000 BTU
  • Colors Orange, red and blue
