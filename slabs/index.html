Book match

Book match

Two slabs are mirror images of each other. Set side by side, the veining opens up like the pages of a book. It is a great fit for a feature wall, a fireplace or the back wall of a shower.

Vein match

The slabs are designed so the veining carries from one slab into the next. Across a long wall or a run of countertops, it reads as one continuous piece.

Matching is available in select finishes and thicknesses. We will confirm the options for your slab when we quote.
